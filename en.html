Parallel strings are calculated as total cells ÷ cells in series. To keep a uniform series-parallel layout and the exact total, the total cell count must be evenly divisible by the series count. Series cells add voltage; parallel strings add amp-hour capacity.

Pack layout and ratings are estimates based on identical modules and nominal values. Actual voltage varies across charge and discharge. This tool does not assess current, thermal behavior, protection, balancing, mechanical design or safety; validate the design with qualified engineering.

NMC tends to excel at

Higher cell energy density and compact, lightweight designs—useful when range or space is a priority.

LFP tends to excel at

Longer cycle life, thermal stability and lower-cost materials—often chosen for frequent cycling and stationary storage.

Indicative ranges compiled from commonly reported commercial-cell and electrode values; they are not guaranteed specifications. Cathode active-material capacity (mAh/g) is not the same as whole-cell capacity (Ah), and neither alone determines pack performance. ¹Cycle life depends strongly on depth of discharge, temperature, charge rate and end-of-life criterion.
